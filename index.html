The cat that grew out of the forest

The Maine Coon is one of the largest domestic cat breeds. Lynx-like ear tufts, a tail like a squirrel’s, and the temperament of a big, gentle dog.

See the participants’ cats

Where they come from

The breed took shape on farms in the northeastern United States, in the state of Maine. Long winters did their work: a thick, water-resistant coat, wide paws that stay on top of the snow, and a bushy tail the cat wraps around itself while sleeping. In 1985 the Maine Coon became the official state cat of Maine.

Just how big is it

Length from nose to tip of tail:

Average house cat ≈ 46 cm
Typical Maine Coon up to 100 cm
Stewie, record holder 123 cm

Males weigh 6–9 kg, females 4–6 kg. They mature slowly and only reach full size at 3–5 years.

What makes them special

Voice
Instead of a loud “meow”, they often make a soft trilling chirp that sounds almost like a bird.
Water
Many Maine Coons aren’t afraid of water and love splashing a paw in their bowl.
Temperament
They follow their people from room to room and get along well with kids and dogs.
Grooming
Brush the coat once or twice a week to keep mats from forming.
